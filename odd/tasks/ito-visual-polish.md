## Design direction
For a Bureau Veritas contact evaluating operational clarity: compact toolbar, brand rail serving content, full-width aligned workspace, clear visit→finding→correction→verification relationships. Neutral titles/active counts, warning/destructive emphasis for overdue/critical, blue primarily for actions/brand. Meaningful restrained icons. Contextual dialogs preserve parent view and permit keyboard/focus-safe operation. Filters collapse into controls with removable chips. Analysis has deliberate centered empty state and staged simulated progress/gradient accents, not fabricated external AI. Wide primary finding content with narrow chronology/actions, realistic simulated evidence form colocated with evidence.
